My Reflection

Thoughts on growth, challenges, and learning

My Growth

The most significant growth I've experienced while learning computer science is in the way I approach problem-solving. Initially, when errors occurred, I would panic or feel like giving up. However, now I enjoy the process of finding the root cause and resolving issues step by step. Through this journey, I've developed both perseverance and logical thinking skills.

Challenges and Learning

The greatest challenge was learning new technologies. Since programming languages and web development were entirely new fields for me, they felt unfamiliar and difficult. However, as I created and modified projects directly, my skills gradually improved. In particular, learning that a single small mistake can affect an entire program taught me the importance of attention to detail and precision.

Memorable Experiences

Creating this portfolio website has been one of the most meaningful experiences. It went beyond simply writing code; I had to consider design, structure, and user experience. Through this project, I learned that technology and creativity can work together seamlessly.

My Areas of Interest

I have interests in life sciences, engineering, and art. While studying AP Biology and AP Computer Science, I developed scientific thinking skills, and through digital art, I've expressed my creativity. Though these fields seem different, they share something in common: the ability to create new ideas and solve problems.

Future Goals

In the future, I want to study bioengineering. My goal is to contribute to research and development that combines life science and technology to improve people's lives. Additionally, I want to continue improving my programming skills and tackle increasingly larger and more meaningful projects.

Lessons Learned

Through this process, I've learned that consistency is more important than perfection. You don't need to be excellent at everything from the start. What truly matters is having the willingness to learn and the courage to keep challenging yourself.
